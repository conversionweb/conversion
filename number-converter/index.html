Number scale converter — thousands, millions and billions

Financial reports, population figures and budget lines are written in whatever scale fits the page. This converter restates one number on every scale so you never count zeros again.

How to use it

  1. 1

    Type into any row — say millions or thousands. The results appear immediately, no button needed.

  2. 2

    Need a combined figure? Fill in two rows, for example millions and units, and they are added together before converting.

  3. 3

    Set how many digits you want (4–12) and click a result to copy the plain number.

Exact conversion factors

These are the definitions the calculator uses. Nothing here is rounded for convenience — where a value is exact by definition, it says so.

  • 1 thousand = 10³ · 1 million = 10⁶ · 1 billion = 10⁹ · 1 trillion = 10¹²
  • 1 billion = 1000 millions (short scale, used here)
  • 1 lakh = 100 thousands = 10⁵ · 1 crore = 10 millions = 10⁷
  • Long scale, still used in parts of Europe, calls 10¹² a billion
Formulaunits = thousands × 10³ = millions × 10⁶ = billions × 10⁹ = trillions × 10¹²

How this converter works

Type 2.5 in the billions row and read 2500 in millions, 2 500 000 in thousands and 2 500 000 000 in units. Because entries are summed, mixed statements work too: 3 millions plus 250 thousands gives 3 250 000.

The scale used here is the short scale — a billion is a thousand million — which is standard in English-language finance and journalism. Some European sources use the long scale, where a billion means a million million; if a translated document looks a thousand times off, that is usually why.

Notes and abbreviations

  • Short scale: thousand 10³, million 10⁶, billion 10⁹, trillion 10¹²
  • Long scale (older European usage): a billion means 10¹²
  • Useful for reading budgets, market caps and population tables

Questions

How many millions in a billion?

One thousand, on the short scale used here.

How do I write 7.4 billion in full?

Type 7.4 in the billions row: 7 400 000 000 units.

Does it handle lakh and crore?

Not as named rows. A lakh is 100 thousands and a crore is 10 millions — enter them that way.
