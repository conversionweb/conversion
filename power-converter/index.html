How this converter works

There are two horsepowers in common use and they differ by about 1.4 percent. Mechanical horsepower (hp) is 745.7 W and appears in British and American figures; metric horsepower (PS, ch, pk) is 735.5 W and is what most European manufacturers quote. A car sold as 150 PS is 148 hp — the same engine, a different yardstick.

BTU per hour is included for climate equipment, where a "12 000 BTU" unit means 12 000 BTU/h, or 3.52 kW of cooling. Reading it as energy rather than power is a common and expensive mistake in quotes.

Notes and abbreviations

  • 1 hp (mechanical) = 745.70 W; 1 PS (metric) = 735.50 W
  • 1 kW = 1.341 hp = 1.360 PS
  • BTU/h ratings on air conditioners are power, not energy

Questions

How many kW is 200 hp?

149.14 kW using mechanical horsepower, or 147.10 kW if the figure was quoted in metric PS.

Which horsepower does my car use?

European and Japanese brochures usually quote PS, UK and US ones hp. A round metric number is a good sign it is PS.

What size air conditioner is 3.5 kW?

11 942 BTU/h, sold as a 12 000 BTU unit.
